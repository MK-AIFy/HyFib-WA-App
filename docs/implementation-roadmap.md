# Implementation Roadmap

## Phase 0 (2 weeks)

- Legal/policy mapping and threat model.
- Domain model and SLO/SLI definitions.
- Infrastructure bill of materials.

## Phase 1 (3 weeks)

- Base infrastructure and identity.
- Tenant and audit foundations.
- Secret management and observability baseline.

## Phase 2 (4 weeks)

- WhatsApp adapter, webhook ingestion, onboarding UI/API.
- Template sync and campaign engine v1.

## Phase 3 (3 weeks)

- Sales inbox and assignment/routing.
- Commerce events integration.
- Analytics dashboards and export workflows.

## Phase 4 (2 weeks)

- Claude backoffice intelligence services.
- Prompt governance and fallback paths.

## Phase 5 (2 weeks)

- Security hardening and performance tuning.
- DR rehearsal and production readiness review.

## Phase 6 (ongoing)

- Controlled tenant rollout.
- KPI and policy tuning.
- Quarterly compliance evidence cycles.
